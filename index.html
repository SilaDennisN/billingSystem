FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before signing up.

Which MikroTik models are supported?
We support MikroTik routers capable of running WireGuard — starting from the RB951Ui and any more powerful model. This includes the RB series (RB1100, RB3011, RB4011), CCR (Cloud Core Router), CRS series, and CHR (Cloud Hosted Router). Entry-level models below RB951Ui, such as the hAP mini or RB750r2, are not supported.
How does M-Pesa integration work?
We integrate with Safaricom Daraja API via your Paybill or Till number. Payments are detected in real time and users are activated within seconds. The payment gateway add-on is KES 400/month.
Can I manage both hotspot and PPPoE?
Yes. All accounts support both hotspot and PPPoE (username/password) networks from a single dashboard — no tier restrictions.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — all accounts include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Explore the full system before committing.
How is the 5% platform fee calculated?
The 5% fee is applied to your total monthly billed income processed through the platform — hotspot and PPPoE included. It is billed at the end of each month based on actual collected revenue.
Can my resellers manage customers?
Yes. You can create reseller accounts with limited access so they can manage assigned customers without seeing your full dashboard or income reports.
